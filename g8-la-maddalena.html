English summary

Role: Geometra · Site Technical Coordination and Survey Control

Refurbishment and construction works at the former naval dockyard in La Maddalena within the G8 infrastructure programme.

  • Site set-out, geometric checks and verification of structural/civil works.
  • Technical support for site activities and construction coordination.
  • Collaboration with site management and field crews during construction.

Photos document the development. The news video is a retrospective on the site, NOT footage of the candidate’s work.
