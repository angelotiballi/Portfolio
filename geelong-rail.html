Role: Senior Engineering Surveyor · Rail Infrastructure Support

Rail duplication programme with associated civil works, rail bridges, station upgrades and level crossing removals.

  • Survey set-out and geometric verification on railway-associated civil infrastructure.
  • As-built surveys and support for technical documentation.
