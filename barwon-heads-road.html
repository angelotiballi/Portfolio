English summary

Role: Senior Engineering Surveyor · Road and Drainage Works Support

Road widening and upgrade in Geelong, with additional lanes, intersections, drainage and shared-use paths.

  • Set-out and verification for earthworks, pavements, drainage and utilities.
  • Level and geometry checks for civil and structural works, including bridges and piles.
  • As-built, QA and conformance records for site handover.

Stage 1 photos and a later Stage 2 progress video are official project material; neither identifies specific work personally performed.
