English summary

Role: Engineering Surveyor · Construction Technical Support

Major road infrastructure linking Melbourne’s north-east freeway network, including twin road tunnels and associated connections.

  • Construction set-out and geometric control against drawings and digital design models.
  • Level, alignment and tolerance checks; as-built surveys for quality records.
  • Daily technical coordination with engineers, supervisors and construction crews.
